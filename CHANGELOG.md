## 0.18.x · The Laurel look

### 0.18.5

- **Vexa is a little weaker:** base attack damage 36 → 33. She was winning about 59% of bot games; in testing this brings her to about 54%.

### 0.18.4

- **A new app icon in 3D:** the same tower split between the blue and red teams by a glowing ember crack, now made of rough stone, with team banners and a fire burning on top at night. To see it on your iPhone, remove Ovra from the home screen and add it again.

### 0.18.3

- **The Attack and spell buttons moved back a little to the left,** between where they were in 0.18.1 and 0.18.2.
- **The level-up + buttons stay still** instead of pulsing.
- **The level-up + is on top of its spell button again.** The new gold rim was covering it.

### 0.18.2

- **The Attack and spell buttons moved further right:** twice as far as in 0.18.1, so they now sit close to the edge of the screen.

### 0.18.1

- **The Attack button, the spell buttons and their level-up buttons moved a little to the right,** so they have more space from the bottom panel.

### 0.18.0

- **A new look for the whole game:** dark iron, polished gold, a gold laurel wreath and small rubies. Every frame was sculpted in 3D and lit, so the metal really shines.
- **Your hero's picture sits in a gold laurel medallion.** The picture is a little smaller to make room, and the health and mana bars moved right so nothing touches.
- **The bottom panel** has a gold rim with a ruby in each corner and more room inside, so items keep clear of the edge. Item slots are sunken iron sockets.
- **The shop button** now sits neatly under the courier button, level with the lower row of items.
- **Top bar:** taller iron plates with space around each hero, square hero pictures, and a gold medallion for the clock.
- **The minimap** has a gold frame. The shield and ping buttons moved a little to the right to keep clear of it.
- **Spell, Attack and other round buttons** all have the same gold rim. Same size and place as before.
- **Every other screen matches:** start menu, Play with Friend and the lobby, the draft, settings, the shop, scoreboard, hero stats, pause, teleport map, ping menu, tutorial, practice, spell cards, messages and the end screen.


### 0.17.13

- **Behind the scenes:** the README (the project's front page on GitHub) now describes the game as it is today: Play with Friend, the announcer, the new tutorial, mountains, Mega Creeps, the settings sections and the current controls.

### 0.17.12

- **The tutorial puts you in better places.** Fighting practice now happens in an open clearing in the jungle instead of between trees, and you always stand with open ground around you.
- **You can see what a lesson is talking about.** Buildings and the mountain now show below your hero, the text box is wider and shorter so it covers less, and the Warden lesson takes you inside its house.

### 0.17.11

- **A shorter hero stats panel.** Tap the i on your portrait: three columns for Attack, Defense and Magic, showing only what isn't already on screen. Extras like lifesteal appear once you have them.

### 0.17.10

- **A new tutorial.** Eight short lessons you can play in any order: the goal, moving and looking, fighting, gold and items, buildings, the map, totems and sight, and the Warden. Finished lessons get a tick. It works with every hero now.
- **Mega Creeps wear armor:** layered dark plate with gold edges and a strip of team colour. Casters get a metal circlet.

### 0.17.9

- **All towers are stronger:** about 20% more health and 15% more damage, Shrine towers too.
- **New item and spell descriptions:** name, mana and cooldown per level with your current level in bold, then Stats, then Active or Passive. Stats are short lines like "+45 Damage".
- Spell numbers that showed odd internal names now have plain names.

### 0.17.4

### 0.17.2

### 0.17.1

- **A war horn** now sounds three seconds before the battle begins.
- **A little piece of music** plays when your hero dies.
- **A short song and the Shrine's explosion** now play at the end of the match.

### 0.17.0

- **Every sound in the game is new.** Natural recordings, layered and shaped: deep rumbling explosions and ground smashes, real rock and wood hits, soft chimes for healing, a choir and harp for teleporting, modelled water for Kaela. Nothing sounds electric any more.
- **All sounds are about the same loudness.** A sword no longer hurts next to a quiet sound.
- **Hits land on time.** A melee sound's impact now plays at the moment the swing visibly connects, for every hero and creature.
- **No more late sounds.** Everything loads before the menu and plays at once. If the phone pauses its audio for a moment, sounds are skipped instead of coming out late.
- A cancelled teleport's sound now fades straight away.
- Using a Herbal Poultice now has its own soft sound.

### 0.16.0
*2026-11-06*

**You can now play with friends:**
- Tap Play with Friend, then Host a game. Friends enter the 4 letter code to join.
- Tap a seat in the lobby to change team or lane. Bots fill the empty seats, and the host sets how strong they are.
- Everyone drafts together, then plays the match.
- If someone leaves, their hero goes home and stands inactive at base. They can come back with Rejoin your last match and carry on with the same hero.

- If the host leaves or locks their phone, the match stops for everyone.
- Online is on the same screen, locked for now.

**Behind the scenes:** the simulation now uses its own math, so a match plays out exactly the same on every phone.

## 0.14.x · New controls and rules

### 0.14.0
*2026-11-05*

**Attacking works in a new way:**
- **Auto attack on:** your hero hits the nearest target in range by itself, also while walking, and stays on it while it's in range. Pick another enemy by holding Attack and dragging: it comes first until it dies or leaves your range. Auto attack never denies and doesn't wake up jungle camps.
- **Auto attack off:** one attack per tap. Holding Attack only aims, and the attack happens when you let go. Tapping faster than your attack speed does nothing extra.
- **Denies** are always one attack, in both modes.

**Teleporting:**
- A bar over your health bar shows anything charging up: teleports and long spell wind-ups.
- Moving no longer cancels a teleport. Your hero stays frozen until it lands, or until you tap the new X on the teleport slot.
- If the tower you're teleporting to falls, you still arrive there.

**New items:**
- **Wayfarer Boots:** the fastest boots. You always have 1 teleport that's never used up, with half the cooldown, and you can teleport to allied heroes and creeps too. You can't buy scrolls while you have them; drop them and you keep 1.
- **Clearwater Flask:** restores mana over time.
- **Long Scope:** a cheap early item: more attack range for ranged heroes, and a little attack speed.

**Also:**
- **Mega Creeps:** when all 6 of a team's barracks have fallen, the enemy sends huge horned creeps that are far stronger.
- The camera starts further out, and that's now the furthest it goes: you can only zoom in.
- The minimap marks jungle camps, and shows couriers out on the map as small dots.
- Joystick sizes are further apart, and the joystick shows on screen when you pick a size in Settings.

## 0.13.x · Three new heroes

### 0.13.0
*2026-11-05*

**Morra the Beastcaller**, a summoner:
- **Call the Pack:** calls a wolf, or two from level 3, that fights for you for 22 seconds.
- **Feral Bond:** your wolves heal, hit harder and run faster. Morra gets the speed too, unless she is the Red Wolf.
- **Red Wolf (ultimate):** for 14 seconds Morra becomes a big red wolf and fights in melee, with more attack speed, damage and move speed and a small chance of critical hits.

**Seren the Aegis**, a sturdy protector:
- **Aegis:** a shield on an ally or herself soaks up damage for 3 seconds.
- **Rebuke:** a lance of light damages and slows everything in its line.
- **Sanctuary (ultimate):** a golden dome: allies inside take much less damage and enemies inside are slowed.

**Rhen the Galewalker**, a fast ranged fighter:
- **Gale Step:** dashes straight through enemies, then attacks faster for 3 seconds.
- **Hawk Mark:** an enemy loses armor and can't hide, even by turning invisible.
- **Bolt Storm (ultimate):** fires a stream of bolts at nearby enemies while Rhen keeps moving.

Also:
- The draft screen shows all 9 heroes in one row.
- Grom hits a little harder (64 starting damage, was 60).

## 0.12.x · New items

### 0.12.0
*2026-11-05*

Eight new items:

- **Sunder Pike:** your attacks lower the target's armor.
- **Storm Blade:** attacks sometimes throw lightning at the target and up to 2 enemies near it.
- **Siege Maul:** your attacks hit buildings much harder.
- **Ghost Shroud:** use it to turn invisible and move faster for a few seconds.
- **Bramble Mail:** melee attackers take part of the damage they deal to you.
- **Mender's Censer:** use it to heal yourself and allied heroes nearby.
- **Hush Bell:** use it to stop nearby enemy heroes casting abilities for 3 seconds. Items still work, and spell immunity protects them.
- **Mire Staff:** use it on an enemy for damage and a heavy slow.

Bots use most of them too. Hero win rates stayed balanced, all between 45% and 55% over 120 bot matches.
